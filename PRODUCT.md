# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two people playing a private, real-time Battleship match from a phone or computer.

## Product Purpose

Create or join a private room, position a fleet, play turn-based Battleship online, and optionally start a rematch.

## Operating Context

Short competitive sessions, primarily on mobile, with a shared room code and live opponent state.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve gameplay, Firebase logic, networking, room flow, and all existing copy.
- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with Firebase modules loaded in the browser.
- The board is a 10 × 10 interactive grid and must remain precise and usable by touch.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Batalha Naval.
- Tactical naval atmosphere.
- Must feel like a premium strategy game rather than a dashboard.

## Evidence on Hand

The working application, its interface copy, icon, manifest, Firebase integration, and game-state renderers are present in the repository. No commercial claims or external brand assets are available.

## Product Principles

- The current game state and next action must be immediately legible.
- Competitive atmosphere should increase immersion without reducing operational clarity.
- Mobile ergonomics and accessible interaction are first-class requirements.
- Preserve product behavior while allowing a complete visual replacement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Strong focus visibility, high contrast, touch-friendly targets, reduced-motion support, and readable typography are required.
