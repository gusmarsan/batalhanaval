# Design System

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction

Pre-dawn command bridge. The interface feels like a physical naval operations station before sunrise: deep graphite metal below, a cobalt horizon through the viewport, and controlled amber/white instrument light. It is cinematic without becoming ornamental.

## Composition

Horizontal horizon bands organize the experience. Critical state and next action live closest to the bright horizon; supporting controls recede into the darker console. The battle board is the dominant operational surface.

## Color

Near-black navy is the foundation. Cobalt and cold cyan communicate active targeting and information. Amber is reserved for attention, readiness, and caution. Red is destructive. Green confirms completed actions.

## Typography

Condensed, authoritative display lettering pairs with a highly legible UI sans. Tabular numerals are used for coordinates and room codes.

## Components

Controls are tactile console keys with restrained bevel and press travel. Inputs resemble illuminated instrument fields. Cards are structural console plates, not repeated floating tiles. Focus states are prominent and keyboard-visible.

## Motion

The opening horizon develops once; radar and pending targeting use calm, continuous physical motion. All interaction motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
